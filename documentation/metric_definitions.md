# Business Metric Definition Register

This document outlines the analytical formulas, definitions, and rules applied throughout this project. It establishes alignment between business logic and the SQL queries.

---

## 1. Revenue Metrics

We define three distinct tiers of revenue. Our default for all analysis is **Recognized Revenue**:

### A. Recognized Revenue (Primary)
- **Definition**: The total value of successfully completed orders.
- **SQL Formula**: 
  $$\text{Recognized Revenue} = \sum (\text{order\_items.quantity} \times \text{order\_items.unit\_price}) \quad \text{where } \text{orders.order\_status} = \text{'Delivered'}$$
- **Business Implication**: This is the standard for accounting and recognized revenue. It ignores pending bookings and removes cancellations.

### B. Net Revenue (Pipeline)
- **Definition**: The total value of active transactions (in-transit + delivered).
- **SQL Formula**: 
  $$\text{Net Revenue} = \sum (\text{order\_items.quantity} \times \text{order\_items.unit\_price}) \quad \text{where } \text{orders.order\_status} \in (\text{'Delivered'}, \text{'Pending'})$$
- **Business Implication**: Useful for inventory allocation and shipping pipeline capacity planning.

### C. Gross Booking Value (GBV)
- **Definition**: The total value of all bookings placed, regardless of outcome.
- **SQL Formula**: 
  $$\text{Gross Booking Value} = \sum (\text{order\_items.quantity} \times \text{order\_items.unit\_price})$$
- **Business Implication**: Measures gross booking interest and marketing pull, but overstates realized revenue if cancellation rates are high.

## 2. Customer Activity & Churn Metrics

### A. Customer Taxonomy

```text
               Total Registered Customers (90)
                /                         \
    Ordering Cohort (80)            Never-Purchased (10)
      /              \
Active (72)       Inactive (8)
                    \
                 Churn-Risk (5)
```

- **Registered Customers**: Total entries in the `customers` database ($N=90$).
- **Ordering Customers (Cohort)**: Customers with at least one order ($N=80$).
- **Never-Purchased Customers**: Registered users with zero orders ($N=10$).
- **Active Customers**: Ordering customers who placed at least one delivered order within the last 90 days.
- **Inactive Customers**: Ordering customers whose last order date is $\le \text{Anchor Date} - 90 \text{ days}$.
- **Churn-Risk Customers**: Ordering customers whose last order date is $\le \text{Anchor Date} - 120 \text{ days}$.

### B. Analytical Churn Rate
- **Definition**: The percentage of purchasing customers who have become inactive.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Churn Rate} = \frac{\text{Ordering Customers with Last Purchase } \le \text{ Anchor Date } - 90 \text{ days}}{\text{Total Ordering Customers}}$$
- **Business Implication**: Evaluates retention health. Never-purchased customers are excluded from the denominator to isolate post-purchase attrition from activation friction.

### C. Never-Purchased Rate
- **Definition**: The percentage of registered users who have never placed an order.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Never-Purchased Rate} = \frac{\text{Never-Purchased Customers}}{\text{Total Registered Customers}}$$
- **Business Implication**: Measures registration-to-first-order dropoff. Highlights acquisition funnel leaks.

## 3. Engagement & Value Metrics

### A. Average Order Value (AOV)
- **Definition**: Average value generated per successful transaction.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{AOV} = \frac{\text{Total Recognized Revenue}}{\text{Total Unique Delivered Orders}}$$
- **Business Implication**: Tracks basket size and purchasing power. Used to set promotion bounds.

### B. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV - Historical)
- **Definition**: Cumulative recognized revenue generated by a single customer.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Historical CLV} = \sum (\text{quantity} \times \text{unit\_price}) \text{ per customer id for Delivered orders}$$
- **Business Implication**: Identifies VIP segments. Tracks customer equity. This is historical revenue, not predictive CLV.

### C. Repeat Purchase Rate (RPR)
- **Definition**: Percentage of active purchasers who buy more than once.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{RPR} = \frac{\text{Customers with } >1 \text{ Delivered Orders}}{\text{Total Purchasing Customers}}$$
- **Business Implication**: Indicates product stickiness and repurchase habits.

## 4. RFM Segmentation Scoring

- **Recency (R)**: Days since last order relative to the dynamic anchor date (`MAX(order_date)`).
  - Score 5: Top 20% (Most recent)
  - Score 1: Bottom 20% (Longest inactive)
- **Frequency (F)**: Count of delivered orders.
  - Score 5: Top 20% (Highest transaction count)
  - Score 1: Bottom 20% (Fewest transactions)
- **Monetary (M)**: Sum of recognized spending (CLV).
  - Score 5: Top 20% (Highest total spend)
  - Score 1: Bottom 20% (Lowest total spend)

### Segment Definitions
- **Champions**: $R \ge 4$, $F \ge 4$, $M \ge 4$
- **Loyal Customers**: $R \ge 3$, $F \ge 3$, $M \ge 3$ (excluding Champions)
- **New Customers**: $R \ge 4$, $F = 1$
- **At Risk**: $R \le 2$, $F \ge 3$, $M \ge 3$
- **Hibernating**: $R \le 2$, $F \le 2$, $M \le 2$
- **Potential Loyalists**: $R \ge 3$, $F \ge 2$ (excluding above categories)
- **About to Sleep**: All others
